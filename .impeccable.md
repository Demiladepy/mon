## Design Context

### Users
Small-business operators in Nigeria and the developers or finance teams who supervise AI-assisted cash operations. They use Rein to see money arrive through Monnify, let an assistant handle routine payouts, review held actions, and understand why risky actions were blocked.

### Brand Personality
Technical, precise, uncompromising. The product should communicate operational competence and financial trust without sounding academic, defensive, or fear-driven.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light, high-trust financial product influenced by Coinbase's clarity, scale, restrained blue, strong typography, and generous spacing—but not a clone. The primary experience is the working operations dashboard, supported by a polished marketing entry point. The memorable element is a live "money path": collection received, agent decision, name inquiry, and payout represented as one legible operational sequence.

Avoid generic cyber-security visuals, neon-on-dark AI styling, excessive cards, glassmorphism, gradient text, and developer-terminal clichés.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with useful cash operations; security appears as proof within the workflow.
2. Make every financial state and policy decision understandable in seconds.
3. Use restrained, sponsor-compatible financial polish with Nigerian business context.
4. Keep the key demo path deterministic, visible, and stage-readable.
5. Preserve accessibility, reduced-motion support, responsive behavior, and honest status language.
